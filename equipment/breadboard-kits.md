## What's Inside the Breadboard Kit

The Breadboard Kit is intended for **second-year students in the TEI (Tangible and Embodied Interaction) course** and for **Master's students** working with physical interaction, prototyping, and embedded systems.

The kit includes:

- 1x [Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2](https://docs.arduino.cc/hardware/nano-33-ble-sense-rev2/)
- 1x [Full-size solderless breadboard](https://www.adafruit.com/product/239)
- 1x USB-A to Micro-USB cable
- 1x set of [male-to-male jumper wires](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1957)
- 1x [NeoPixel Ring](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1643)
- 1x Micro Servo Motor
- Assorted potentiometers
- Assorted push buttons
- 1x Piezo Buzzer
- Assorted LEDs

> **Important:** Standard LED rings are relatively fragile and tend to fail more often than other components. If a ring does not light up, first try another LED and check its polarity before troubleshooting the Arduino or breadboard.
